## Notes

- **Discord** is the most generous free option: unlimited messages, history, and voice -- ideal for developer communities and open-source projects.
- **Notion** is excellent for solo knowledge management but restrictive for team collaboration on the free plan.
- **Linear** has a hard cap of 250 issues on free, making it suitable only for proof-of-concept or small side projects.
- **ClickUp** packs the most features into its free tier (tasks, time tracking, Gantt) but has tight storage limits (100 MB).
- **Slack** vs **Discord**: Slack has a polished business feel but 90-day message history; Discord has unlimited history but a more casual interface.
