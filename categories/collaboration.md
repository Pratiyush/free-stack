## Notes

- **Discord** remains the most generous free option: unlimited messages, history, and voice -- ideal for developer communities and open-source projects.
- **Lark** (international version of Feishu by ByteDance) is a hidden gem: 200 GB storage, unlimited video calls for 350 people, built-in docs/sheets, and AI features -- all free. The 20-member cap is the main constraint.
- **Twist** (by the Todoist team) is purpose-built for async-first teams. Threaded conversations organized by topic replace real-time chat. The 1-month history limit on free is the main drawback.
- **Basecamp** brought back a free tier but it is extremely limited (1 project, 1 GB). Best for students/nonprofits who get full access free.
- **Notion** is excellent for solo knowledge management but restrictive for team collaboration on the free plan (1,000-block cap for multi-member workspaces).
- **Linear** has a hard cap of 250 issues on free, making it suitable only for proof-of-concept or small side projects.
- **ClickUp** packs the most features into its free tier (tasks, time tracking, Gantt) but has tight storage limits (100 MB).
- **Slack** vs **Discord**: Slack has a polished business feel but 90-day message history; Discord has unlimited history but a more casual interface.
