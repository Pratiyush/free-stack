## Notes

- None of these services charge monthly fees -- all are pay-per-transaction or revenue-based.
- **Merchant of Record (MoR)** services (LemonSqueezy, Paddle, Creem, Polar) handle global tax compliance, which is invaluable for indie developers selling internationally.
- **Creem** stands out with the lowest MoR rate (3.9% + 40c) plus a 0% introductory period on the first 1,000 EUR.
- **RevenueCat** is specifically for mobile in-app subscriptions (iOS/Android) and is genuinely free under $2,500/mo revenue.
- **Stripe** has the lowest per-transaction rate but leaves tax compliance to you.
