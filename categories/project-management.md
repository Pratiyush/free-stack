## Notes

- **Permanent free tiers**: All seven services listed offer permanent free tiers with no time restrictions.
- **Unlimited users**: Plane and Huly stand out by offering unlimited users on their free tiers, unlike the 10-15 user caps on other services.
- **Open source options**: Plane, Taiga, and Huly are all open source and can be self-hosted for free with no restrictions.
- **YouTrack** offers the most generous storage (30 GB) and full feature parity with paid tiers for up to 10 users.
- **Jira** remains the industry standard but has the most constrained free tier (2 GB storage, 100 automation runs/mo).
- **Shortcut** and **Height** are developer-focused alternatives designed specifically for engineering teams.
- **Huly** is noteworthy as an all-in-one platform combining project management, documents, chat, and video -- all free with unlimited users.
