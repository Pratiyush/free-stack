## Notes

- **GitHub** Actions minutes are unlimited for public repositories; 2,000 min/mo applies only to private repos on free plan.
- **GitLab** 5-user limit applies only to private top-level groups on GitLab.com; self-hosted GitLab CE is fully free and open source.
- **Bitbucket** workspace storage limit was reduced to 1 GB starting April 2025; previously it was larger.
- **Linear** free tier caps at 250 active issues with a hard limit -- no grace period or overage.
- **Postman** eliminated free team plans in March 2026; free is now single-user only.
- **Insomnia** is open source (MIT); the free tier has full local functionality but no cloud sync/collaboration.
- **Tailscale** Personal plan is explicitly for non-commercial, personal use only.
